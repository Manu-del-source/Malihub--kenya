# Support and Report Handling

## Before launch

- [ ] Publish a support contact channel that is actively monitored.
- [ ] Assign an owner and backup for safety reports.
- [ ] Confirm who can access reports and who may restrict or remove content.
- [ ] Test report submission from a signed-in account and confirm the report is persisted.
- [ ] Test invalid listing IDs, repeated submissions, and unexpected errors.
- [ ] Confirm how reviewers find new reports and record outcomes.
- [ ] Define a response target only if staffing can meet it.
- [ ] Keep a minimal audit trail of decisions without copying passwords, payment PINs, or unnecessary personal data.

## Triage

1. Check the reported listing and stated reason.
2. Assess immediate safety, fraud, counterfeit, prohibited-item, and privacy risks.
3. Preserve only the evidence needed to review the case.
4. Decide whether to take no action, request more information, restrict/remove content, or escalate.
5. Record the outcome and rationale in the approved internal system.
6. Communicate with the reporter only through an approved, monitored channel.

## Current limitations

The website has a listing-report dialog and server action, but the presence of this UI alone does not prove there is an operational administrator review queue or resolution workflow. Verify persistence and staff access before describing moderation as fully operational. The report flow requires sign-in. Do not invent response-time commitments or claim all listings are verified.
