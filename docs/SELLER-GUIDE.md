# MaliHub Seller Guide

## Getting started

1. Create a MaliHub account and choose the seller flow.
2. Complete the seller profile using accurate information.
3. Add clear, current photos and truthful descriptions for each item.
4. Set a price and keep stock/availability information current.
5. Respond to buyer enquiries and agree on delivery or collection details before a transaction.

## Listing quality

- Use your own photos where possible and make sure they represent the item being sold.
- Describe size, condition, material, colour, defects, and included accessories accurately.
- State the price clearly and update the listing when the item is sold or unavailable.
- Do not list counterfeit, stolen, illegal, dangerous, or prohibited goods.
- Do not claim that MaliHub has verified an item unless the platform explicitly displays that status.

## Orders, delivery, and communication

Use only order, payment, and delivery features that are actually shown as available in the app. Confirm who is responsible for delivery, the expected timing, any delivery cost, and the final amount before accepting an arrangement. Never ask a buyer for their M-Pesa PIN or one-time verification code.

## Fees during the launch

Do not promise that MaliHub will remain free forever. Any platform fee or charge should be communicated before a user confirms the relevant paid action. Follow the current on-site terms and any official launch announcement.

## Safety and support

Buyers can report a suspicious listing from its product detail page using **Report listing**. Reporting currently requires a signed-in account. See [Safety and Reporting](./SAFETY-AND-REPORTING.md) for guidance.
