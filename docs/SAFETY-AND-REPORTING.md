# Safety and Reporting

## Current user-facing reporting flow

1. Open a product detail page.
2. Select **Report listing**.
3. Choose one of the available reasons: suspected scam, counterfeit item, prohibited item, misleading listing, offensive content, or other.
4. Add optional context and submit.

The current report action requires the user to be signed in. Anonymous reporting is not supported by this UI. The interface confirms receipt of a report; that confirmation must not be described as a finding that the listing is fraudulent or that it has been removed.

## Triage guidance

When reviewing a report, record the report identifier, listing, reason, received time, and review outcome in the approved internal system. Review the listing and relevant evidence before taking action. Do not promise a resolution time unless an operational SLA has been adopted.

Possible outcomes should be documented consistently: no action, request more information, restrict or remove a listing, or escalate for further review. Any account or listing action must follow the application's authorization rules and applicable law.

## Safety advice for users

- Never request or disclose an M-Pesa PIN, password, or one-time code.
- Treat screenshots as unverified payment claims; confirm receipt in the actual account.
- Verify item condition, availability, price, and delivery/collection terms before paying.
- Do not list counterfeit, stolen, illegal, dangerous, or otherwise prohibited goods.
- Avoid publishing private personal information in listing text or images.

## Known limitations to validate before launch

- The report UI requires authentication.
- A report submission confirmation does not itself establish that an administrator has reviewed the report.
- Do not advertise a guaranteed moderation turnaround or claim every seller/listing is verified.
- Confirm the report database model, administrator access/review interface, notification process, and resolution workflow before promising operational moderation.
- Publish a real, monitored support contact channel before promoting general support as available.
