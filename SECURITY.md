# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

Security fixes are provided for the latest version of MaliHub Kenya on the `main` branch. Older releases may not receive security updates.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately. **Do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, or social media.**

1. Open the [MaliHub Kenya repository](https://github.com/Manu-del-source/Malihub--kenya).
2. Select the **Security** tab.
3. Choose **Report a vulnerability** to submit a private vulnerability report.

If the private reporting option is unavailable, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Do not publish technical details until the issue has been assessed and a fix or mitigation is available.

When reporting, include as much of the following as you safely can:

- A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- The affected URL, endpoint, component, or version.
- Reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept.
- Any relevant logs, screenshots, or request/response examples with personal data, credentials, tokens, and secrets removed.
- Whether you believe the issue is actively being exploited.

## What to Expect

- We aim to acknowledge reports within **5 business days**.
- We will investigate the report, assess its severity and impact, and provide updates as the investigation progresses.
- We aim to share a remediation plan or status update within **10 business days** of acknowledgement.
- Timelines may vary depending on complexity and severity. We will coordinate disclosure with the reporter where practical.

## Responsible Disclosure

Please:

- Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address the issue before public disclosure.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, deleting, or exposing data that does not belong to you.
- Do not disrupt services, degrade availability, or conduct denial-of-service testing.
- Do not use social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks.
- Test only accounts and resources you own or have explicit permission to assess.
- Stop testing and report immediately if you encounter another person's data.

We appreciate responsible reports that help keep MaliHub Kenya and its users safe.
