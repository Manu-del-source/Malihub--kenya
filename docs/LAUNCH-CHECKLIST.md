# MaliHub Launch Checklist: Support and Safety

## Public experience
- [ ] Support & Safety page is linked from the site footer.
- [ ] Report listing control is visible on product details on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Report reasons and optional details are understandable.
- [ ] Signed-out users receive a clear sign-in instruction.
- [ ] Confirm hello@smartbiz365.site receives mail and is monitored for MaliHub requests.
- [ ] Terms and Privacy pages remain reachable from the footer.

## Operational readiness
- [ ] Verify report persistence in the production database.
- [ ] Verify who can view reports and how they review them.
- [ ] Test a report from submission through a recorded decision.
- [ ] Assign a report owner and backup.
- [ ] Decide and publish response expectations only if sustainable.
- [ ] Check for spam, duplicate reports, and unauthorized access.
- [ ] Document how urgent safety issues are escalated.

## Seller onboarding
- [ ] Seller profile information is accurate.
- [ ] Each seller adds at least five complete, accurate listings where feasible.
- [ ] Seller understands they manage buyer communication and delivery arrangements unless the app explicitly offers another workflow.
- [ ] Seller understands the current fee policy and is not promised permanent free access.
